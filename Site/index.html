What we do

Bring us the database problem nobody has had time to look at.

Most work starts as one of these. Anything from a couple of days to a few weeks, scoped before it starts so you know what you are paying for.

Health checks & risk reviews

A proper look at the estate: configuration, backups, maintenance, security, versions and what would actually happen if a server died.

  • Written findings, ranked by risk
  • Fixes you can hand to your team
  • Backup and restore testing

Performance tuning

For the slow process everyone has learned to live with, and the one that only misbehaves on Monday mornings.

  • Query Store and plan regression analysis
  • Wait, blocking and IO investigation
  • Index and statistics review

HA, DR and resilience

Availability Groups that fail over when you need them to, and a recovery plan somebody has actually tested.

  • Always On AGs and Distributed AGs, WSFC, log shipping
  • Restore testing and DR runbooks
  • Azure Recovery Services Vault

Migrations & upgrades

Moving off an old version, onto new hardware, or into Azure - planned so the cutover is the boring part.

  • SQL Server 2000–2022, environment refreshes
  • SQL Server on Azure IaaS, Azure SQL MI and Azure SQL Database
  • Rollback plans and post-migration checks

Security & permissions audits

Who can do what, where it came from, and what to take away without breaking the application.

  • Permissions and role review
  • Auditing and access history
  • Practical, staged remediation

DBA cover & automation

Short-term cover, backlog clearance, or the scripts and reporting you have never had time to write.

  • Production support and backlog work
  • T-SQL and PowerShell automation
  • Operational reporting: jobs, space, growth, availability

About

Built on seventeen years of other people's production databases.

Molehill Data Services was built on seventeen years of production DBA work - estates where the database going down means the business stops. Recent work has covered SQL Server 2019 and 2022 on Azure IaaS, Availability Groups, Distributed AGs, large production databases and the reporting that keeps everyone honest.

That is the standard every engagement is held to: senior hands on your estate, not a ticket queue. The industry rarely matters - a slow query, a failing backup and an untested failover look much the same whoever owns the database. We work remote-first from North Cambridgeshire, across the UK, and hybrid arrangements are open to discussion depending on the work.
